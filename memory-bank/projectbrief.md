# Brasaland Digital — Project Brief

## Overview: The Business

Brasaland is a grilled-food restaurant chain founded in Medellín, Colombia, in 2008. It operates 14 company-owned locations across Colombia and Florida, employs approximately 115 people, and generates around $6 million in annual revenue. The company is built on consistent food, warm service, and fast kitchens, regardless of location.

As Brasaland has expanded into two countries, its business processes and technology have not kept pace. Teams depend on disconnected point-of-sale systems, spreadsheets, phone calls, WhatsApp, paper forms, and physical loyalty cards. Brasaland Digital was created to build the tools and systems needed to support the company's continued growth without losing its identity.

## Project Objectives

- Build a central API and data platform for locations, menus, sales, customers, and suppliers.
- Provide timely operational and executive dashboards, with sales visibility across locations and currencies (COP and USD).
- Improve the customer experience through digital ordering, loyalty, customer profiles, and personalized offers.
- Centralize supplier and purchasing information to track prices and support better procurement decisions.
- Reduce manual HR work through tools for onboarding, absence management, and workforce reporting.
- Make recipes, training materials, and updates easy to find and distribute consistently across all locations.
- Automate reporting and give leaders better access to business information, including the ability to ask questions in natural language.

## Problems We Will Solve

- **Fragmented information:** Sales, purchasing, staffing, and customer data are spread across separate systems and files, limiting chain-wide visibility.
- **Slow, reactive decisions:** Leaders rely on calls, delayed reports, and intuition instead of timely information about business performance.
- **Inefficient inventory and procurement:** Orders are placed without consolidated purchasing or reliable stock and demand data, contributing to overstock, stockouts, and missed price changes.
- **Disconnected customer experience:** The outdated digital channels and physical stamp-card loyalty programme provide limited convenience and little customer insight.
- **Manual administrative work:** HR processes, shift reporting, and onboarding depend heavily on email, spreadsheets, and paperwork.
- **Inconsistent training and standards:** Hard-to-find materials and slow update distribution make it difficult to keep recipes and procedures consistent across locations and languages.
- **Operational issues detected late:** Without real-time location telemetry, problems such as a location having no sales during opening hours may go unnoticed.

The project will connect Brasaland's data and workflows so teams can operate more efficiently, make informed decisions sooner, and deliver a consistent experience across both markets.
